How It Works

Three simple steps to reunite you with your belongings.

  1. 01

    Report the Item

    Post a lost or found listing with photos, category, and the exact campus location. Takes under 2 minutes.

  2. 02

    Connect & Verify

    Use our real-time chat to communicate. Submit a claim request and verify ownership through our secure process.

  3. 03

    Recover Your Item

    Once verified, coordinate the handoff on campus. The listing is marked Returned and you receive a confirmation email.
